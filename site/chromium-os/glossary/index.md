Welcome to the **Glossary** of terms related to Chromium (3d<sup>5</sup>4s<sup>1</sup>) and his world. This page provides definitions for commonly-used terms and acronyms within Chromium's ecosystem, from his unique nickname to his favorite 2-dim anime titles.

---

## **Key Terms**
- **3d<sup>5</sup>4s<sup>1</sup>:** Chromium's nickname, derived from his academic achievements and the electron configuration of chromium (Cr).  
- **ChromiumOS:** Chromium's own songlist (os), a curated collection of 2-dim tracks that fuel his academic and coding endeavors.  
- **2-dim:** Short for "2-dimensional," a term used to describe anime, manga, and related media.  
- **BGM:** Background music, often used to enhance focus and productivity.  
- **Zone:** A state of deep focus and productivity, often achieved with the help of 2-dim music.  

---

## **Anime Titles**
- **《进击的巨人》 (Attack on Titan):** Known for its intense action and emotional depth, this anime is a favorite of Chromium's.  
- **《新世纪福音战士》 (Neon Genesis Evangelion):** A classic anime with a complex storyline and iconic soundtrack.  
- **《东京喰种》 (Tokyo Ghoul):** A dark and emotional series with a hauntingly beautiful soundtrack.  
- **《火影忍者》 (Naruto):** A long-running anime with themes of perseverance and teamwork.  
- **《你的名字》 (Your Name):** A critically acclaimed film with a memorable and uplifting soundtrack.
