Welcome to the **Contributing** section of ChromiumOS. This page provides a guide for adding or updating content in the library, whether it's a new study technique, a coding tip, or a 2-dim track recommendation. Help expand the ChromiumOS library and share your knowledge with the community.

---

## **How to Contribute**
1. **Fork the Repository:** Start by forking the ChromiumOS repository on GitHub.  
2. **Make Your Changes:** Add your content, whether it's a new track recommendation, a study tip, or a coding tutorial.  
3. **Submit a Pull Request:** Once your changes are complete, submit a pull request for review.  

---

## **Guidelines for Contributions**
- **Relevance:** Ensure your content is relevant to Chromium's world of academics, coding, and 2-dim music.  
- **Accuracy:** Double-check your information to ensure it's accurate and up-to-date.  
- **Clarity:** Write clearly and concisely, so others can easily understand your contributions.
