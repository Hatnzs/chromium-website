Welcome to the **How to Build Your Own Study Playlist** guide. This page will walk you through creating a personalized playlist of 2-dim tracks to boost your focus and productivity, inspired by Chromium's (3d<sup>5</sup>4s<sup>1</sup>) methods.

---

## **Step 1: Choose the Right Tracks**
Start by selecting songs that match your study needs. Here are some categories to consider:
- **High-Energy Tracks:** Perfect for intense study sessions or coding marathons. Examples include **《红莲の弓矢》** (《进击的巨人》主题曲) and **《Silhouette》** (《火影忍者》主题曲).  
- **Emotional Tracks:** Ideal for late-night study sessions or reflective work. Examples include **《unravel》** (《东京喰种》主题曲).  
- **Upbeat Tracks:** Great for brainstorming or reviewing notes. Examples include **《前前前世》** (《你的名字》主题曲).  

---

## **Step 2: Organize Your Playlist**
Arrange your tracks based on the type of task you're working on:
- **Exam Prep:** Use high-energy tracks to stay focused and motivated.  
- **Coding:** Use tracks with a steady rhythm to maintain flow.  
- **Reviewing Notes:** Use upbeat tracks to keep your energy levels high.  

---

## **Step 3: Test and Adjust**
Not every track will work for every task. Experiment with different songs and adjust your playlist as needed. Pay attention to how each track affects your focus and productivity.
