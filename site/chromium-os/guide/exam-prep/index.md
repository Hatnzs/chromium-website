Welcome to the **Exam Prep with Chromium's Techniques** guide. This page will walk you through Chromium's (3d<sup>5</sup>4s<sup>1</sup>) unique strategies for exam preparation, including his use of 2-dim music to enter the "zone."

---

## **Step 1: Create a Study Schedule**
- **Set Goals:** Break down your syllabus into manageable sections.  
- **Allocate Time:** Dedicate specific time slots for each subject.  
- **Include Breaks:** Schedule regular breaks to avoid burnout.  

---

## **Step 2: Use 2-dim Music to Enhance Focus**
- **High-Energy Tracks:** Use tracks like **《红莲の弓矢》** (《进击的巨人》主题曲) for intense study sessions.  
- **Emotional Tracks:** Use tracks like **《unravel》** (《东京喰种》主题曲) for late-night study sessions.  
- **Upbeat Tracks:** Use tracks like **《前前前世》** (《你的名字》主题曲) for reviewing notes.  

---

## **Step 3: Practice Active Recall**
- **Flashcards:** Create flashcards for key concepts and review them regularly.  
- **Mock Exams:** Simulate exam conditions to test your knowledge.  
- **Teach Others:** Explain concepts to friends or classmates to reinforce your understanding.  

---

**Why It Works:**  
2-dim music helps reduce stress and improve focus, while active recall techniques enhance memory retention. By combining these strategies, you can prepare effectively for exams.
